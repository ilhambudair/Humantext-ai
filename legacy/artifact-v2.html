AI writing analysis

The analysis measures the texture of your prose, not its origin. It counts things you can check yourself: how much sentence lengths vary, which phrases repeat, how wide the vocabulary is, how often formulaic connectors appear, how evenly paragraphs are built.

The result is a range, not a verdict. Writing that scores high is often just writing that is very even — which is a fair thing to know about a draft, whoever wrote it.

Natural rewriting

The rewriter works on phrasing, sentence variety and flow. It keeps your meaning, your facts, your numbers, your citations and your terminology exactly where you put them.

It will not invent sources
No citation, statistic or reference is added that was not already in your text.
It will not add mistakes
Deliberate typos and broken grammar do not make writing human. They just make it worse.
It will not promise anything about detectors
The goal is a clearer draft you can defend, not a score on someone else's tool.

Questions

Can this prove who wrote a text?

No, and nothing else can either. It measures stylistic patterns that are common in machine-generated prose, and plenty of human writing shares them — technical documentation, translated text, careful non-native English, anything written to a template. Treat the score as a description of the writing, never as evidence about a writer.

Why did my own writing score high?

Usually because it is very even: similar sentence lengths, a steady supply of connectors like "moreover" and "therefore", paragraphs of near-identical shape. That is a normal result of careful editing. Open "Why this result?" to see which specific patterns drove the number.

Will the rewriter get my text past an AI detector?

That is not what it is for and it is not promised. It improves phrasing, rhythm and readability. If your work needs to be your own, the rewriting still has to be your own decision about every sentence — read the comparison and accept the changes you actually agree with.

How long should the text be?

At least 120 words for a meaningful read, and 300 or more is better. Below that, the statistics are noise and the panel will say so.

Does it work in languages other than English?

The statistical measures are language-agnostic. Phrase-level checks currently know English and Indonesian connector patterns; other languages will simply show fewer phrase findings. Rewriting follows whatever language your text is in.

    About

    What this tool measures, and what it deliberately does not claim.

    HumanText AI is an AI writing assistant for analyzing, improving and personalizing your writing. It is a writing instrument, not a verdict machine. It reads a draft the way an editor skims for habits: does every sentence run the same length, does the same connector open every paragraph, does the vocabulary keep circling the same dozen words.

    How the estimate is built

    The AI-like pattern indicator is a weighted blend of measurements taken from your text. Nothing is learned from a model and nothing is compared against a database — you could reproduce every number by hand with enough patience.

    • Sentence uniformity (24%) — the coefficient of variation of sentence lengths. Low variation is the single strongest pattern in machine-generated prose.
    • Formulaic connectors (20%) — density of phrases like "moreover", "it is important to note", "oleh karena itu", "dengan demikian".
    • Repetition (14%) — repeated three-word sequences, repeated sentence openers, and how concentrated the content words are.
    • Absent human texture (14%) — contractions, questions, asides, first-person marks, short fragments, hedges. Their absence nudges the figure up.
    • Paragraph regularity (8%) — how similar paragraphs are in size and shape.
    • Vocabulary spread (8%) — a moving-window type-token ratio, which does not punish long texts the way a plain ratio does.
    • Triplet phrasing (7%) and dash density (5%) — two habits that models lean on heavily.

    Several of these appearing together counts for more than any one alone, so the blend is nudged up when three or more are strongly elevated. The figure is then bounded to the 5–95 range: this method cannot justify certainty at either end, and a page that printed 0% or 100% would be lying about what it knows.

    Short texts are unreliable and the panel says so rather than hiding it. Under 120 words the figure is pulled toward the middle, because at that length the statistics are mostly noise.

    The other tools

    Paragraph analysis scores each paragraph on the same signals, so you can see which one reads most formulaically and send it straight to the rewriter. Short paragraphs give less reliable readings, and the page says so.

    Readability is estimated from sentence length and how many long words appear. It is a rough guide rather than a formula tied to one language.

    Answer Improver and Student Mode check an answer against its question and tidy it while keeping your ideas, examples, arguments and conclusion. Scores are writing indicators, not grades, and when something important is missing the tool says so instead of inventing it.

    Reference Assistant formats sources you provide and checks that in-text citations match your reference list. It never adds a source and cannot verify that one exists.

    Writing Coach gives feedback so you can improve the draft yourself. It does not rewrite.

    What it cannot do

    It cannot identify an author. It cannot distinguish a careful human editor from a model, because after enough editing the patterns converge — that is the honest limit of every stylometric method, including the commercial ones. False positives fall hardest on non-native writers and on technical prose, both of which are naturally more uniform.

    On using the rewriter honestly

    If the work you are writing is supposed to be yours, a rewrite is a draft to argue with, not an answer to accept. Read the comparison, keep the changes that say what you meant, throw out the ones that do not. Many institutions treat undisclosed AI assistance as misconduct, and that is a question about your rules, not about your word count. The rewriter will never claim your text was written by a human, and it will not degrade your grammar to look more convincing to a detector.
